Seeing LiDAR through a camera.

The final assessment of NVIDIA's course Building AI Agents with Multimodal Models. A LiDAR model could tell cubes from spheres, but LiDAR sensors are rare. How I work with AI.

I use AI to explore ideas, prototype faster and understand how things work. The goal isn't just to generate code — it's to learn from it, test it and make informed decisions. The projects below show where I am on that path.

Define the role like a classBefore a task, I describe who the assistant is, what it knows and how it should teach — properties first, then behaviour.

02compare

Compare, don't assumeThe same prompt gave a full mini-lesson from Claude and a short answer from ChatGPT. Asked in Russian and Uzbek, the wording changed too.

03verify

AI suggests, I decideIn studies of GitHub Copilot, one task was finished 55.8% faster — yet 46% of suggestions changed when a prompt was reworded. So I test and try to understand code before I keep it.
